# Introduction


This service offers a comprehensive and user-friendly solution for managing access to classification properties within Building Information Modeling (BIM) projects. Designed to streamline workflows and enhance project security, our service features an intuitive frontend interface that simplifies the process of access management. Users can effortlessly configure customized access controls tailored to the unique needs of various use-cases and user groups. By leveraging this service, project administrators can ensure that sensitive classification data is accessible only to authorized personnel, thereby maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of the project information. Whether you are working on small-scale projects or large, complex BIM initiatives, this service provides the flexibility and control needed to manage access permissions effectively, promoting efficient collaboration and enhancing overall project management.

## Next Steps for Getting Started

Once you have familiarized yourself with the basic overview of our service, you will be guided through a series of steps to ensure a smooth and efficient setup and utilization of the system. These steps are designed to help you configure and leverage the full capabilities of our access management solution for your BIM projects.

### Quick Setup

In the Quick Setup phase, you will be introduced to the initial configuration process. This step-by-step guide will walk you through the basic settings and essential configurations required to get the service up and running. It will cover how to set up your account, configure initial project settings, and establish basic access controls.

### Keycloak Setup

In the Keycloak Setup step, you will be guided on how to integrate our service with Keycloak, a powerful open-source identity and access management solution. This integration is crucial for managing user authentication and authorization. Detailed instructions will help you configure Keycloak to work seamlessly with our service, ensuring secure and streamlined access management.

### Use Case Service

In the Use Case Service Explained step, you will explore the basic functionality provided for managing use cases. This service allows you to Read, Create, Update, and Delete use cases, offering a simple yet essential set of tools to handle different scenarios within your BIM projects.

### Guideline Service

Finally, the Guideline Service Explained section will cover how to utilize the Guideline Service to upload your classification guidelines. These guidelines will be used to manage access to classifications and their properties effectively. This section will help you understand the importance of guidelines in maintaining structured and secure access control.

By following these steps, you will be well-equipped to manage access to classification properties in your BIM projects efficiently and securely, leveraging the full potential of our comprehensive access management solution.

### Access Service Interface

The Interface section will provide an in-depth look at the user-friendly frontend interface of our service. You will learn how to navigate through various menus, utilize different features, and customize the interface to suit your specific needs. This section ensures that you can efficiently manage access controls with ease and confidence.
